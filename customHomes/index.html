Frequently Asked Questions

How much does custom home design cost?+
Design fees typically range from $5,000–$11,000+, depending on complexity. Discussion during consultation.
How much does a custom home cost to build?+
Construction typically ranges $750,000–$2,000,000+ depending on size, finishes, and location.
How long does the process take?+
Consultation to permits: 12–18 months. Construction: 12–24 months depending on size.
Can you design for multiple generations?+
Yes. Multi-generational design is a specialty. We create homes where multiple generations live comfortably.
Can I include a garden suite?+
Yes. Garden suites are increasingly permitted in Greater Vancouver. Municipal rules vary.
Do I need permits?+
Yes. Custom homes require building permits. We coordinate this and ensure compliance.
Should I renovate or rebuild?+
We analyze both during consultation and recommend the best path for your situation.
What happens after the consultation?+
We send a summary of recommendations and next steps. If you want to proceed, we discuss project details.
